Vampire & Human Dilution Program

The Vampire & Human Dilution Program was established to reduce concentrated vampire populations and encourage greater human-vampire integration throughout the West Coast. Qualifying full-blooded vampires were provided relocation, housing, and living expenses in exchange for permanently settling within participating communities and entering arranged marriages with human partners. The Program is no longer active due to oversaturation in Vampire Territories; however, it was successful in generating substantially reduced modern vampire blood percentages.
