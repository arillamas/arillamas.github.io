Meme Mashup

"GEN Z STARE" This piece is a parody of the " That 2,000-Yard Stare" painting, which doubles as a modern-day meme. I've seen many memes on the internet, and this piece represents all of them: crazy streamers, gambling apps, Trump's countless AI posts, gas prices, whatever new robot has been created, and whatever else is a trending topic. The title "Gen Z Stare" is actually a meme too, referring to "...a blank, deadpan facial expression given during everyday interactions where a verbal response or a friendly smile is normally expected...", which I think is very fitting because we're always joking about these topics online but tend to stray away from discussing them in person.
