Welcome home to Cruz Cove!

As the heart of our diverse community, human residents enjoy everything Cruz Cove has to offer! From our schools and healthcare facilities to bustling businesses, public programs, and beautiful residential neighborhoods, you'll have everything you need right at your doorstep. Living alongside our vampire and werewolf neighbors also means experiencing one of the West Coast's most uniquely diverse communities—all while programs like the Werewolf Removal Protection Law and Vampire Blood Act help ensure your comfort and safety. No special identification, tracking, or residency requirements necessary. Simply find your new home and become part of the Cruz Cove community!
