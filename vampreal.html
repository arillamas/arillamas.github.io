Future Vampire Resident Guidelines

Vampire residents are classified by blood percentage, with modern full-blooded vampires typically possessing 40–60% vampire ancestry and half-blooded vampires approximately 10–30%, largely due to the Vampire & Human Dilution Program. They may freely access human public spaces, businesses, schools, and public resources. However, all vampire residents must permanently reside within designated Vampire Territories, where human spouses are also permitted to live. Only humans are permitted, since marriage between vampires and either full-blooded or half-human werewolves is prohibited. Residents are not required to carry tracking devices, but must tap their identification cards when leaving vampire territory or entering human-zoned areas.

Note: The Vampire Blood Act may make certain government social services unavailable.
