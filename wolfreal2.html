Future Werewolf Resident Guidelines

Full-Blood Werewolf:

Full-blooded werewolves live in designated territories outside of town and must follow strict curfews and carry tracking pagers whenever they leave home. While they may enter town, Human-Only Zones and the Werewolf Removal Protection Law. heavily restrict where they can go and how they behave. They cannot use public human resources, attend public schools, live among humans, or marry humans or vampires. Private human institutions sometimes accept werewolves, though these opportunities are mostly accessible to wealthy and influential families.

Half-Human Werewolf:

Half-human werewolves live in separate territories from full-bloods but are still required to follow curfews and carry tracking pagers. They have greater access to human schools, medical care, public assistance, and certain restricted areas, although those who appear more wolf-like may experience greater discrimination. Half-humans may choose to live permanently among humans, but must give up connections to their werewolf territory and accept increased monitoring. They are also still subject to the Werewolf Removal Protection Law.
