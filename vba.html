Vampire Blood Act

The Vampire Blood Act requires vampire residents to provide documentation of their registered blood percentage when requested by qualifying medical or government facilities outside vampire territory. Facilities may restrict or deny services when an individual's vampire blood percentage is considered a potential safety risk, particularly in environments containing stored human blood. The Act was established following concerns that exposure to human blood could trigger dormant feeding instincts in residents with higher vampire ancestry. Vampire-territory facilities therefore remain the recommended option for vampire residents requiring medical care.
