Future Human Resident Guidelines

Human residents maintain unrestricted residency throughout all designated Human Territories and may freely access public schools, healthcare, government assistance, businesses, and social programs. Humans are not subject to tracking, identification checkpoints, blood-percentage documentation, or species-based curfews. Residents may enter designated Vampire and Werewolf Territories where local access permits, though permanent residency may be subject to territory regulations. Human residents may marry vampires and half-human werewolves; however, unions with full-blooded werewolves are not recognized within Full-Blood Werewolf Territories. Human residents are protected under both the Werewolf Removal Protection Law and Vampire Blood Act, including the right to request removal or refuse certain interactions when established species-safety requirements are met.
