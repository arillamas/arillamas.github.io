Come meet the vampires of Cruz Cove!

Thanks to generations of successful human-vampire integration through local programs, today's vampire residents are more connected with their human neighbors than ever! Vampires enjoy access to human businesses, schools, public programs, and community spaces while sharing their own close-knit Vampire Territories, complete with specialized resources designed around their unique heritage. And no tracking devices here! Residents simply tap their identification cards when traveling outside their territory, helping make travel throughout Cruz Cove safe and convenient for everyone.
