Werewolf Removal Protection Law:

The Werewolf Removal Protection Law allows humans to request that a werewolf be removed from an area if they reasonably believe the werewolf has become threatening or dangerously agitated. Physical violence does not have to occur beforehand, making emotional behavior enough to raise suspicion. The law applies to both full-blooded and half-human werewolves, regardless of how human a half-werewolf may appear.
